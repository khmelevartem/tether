## Why

The pairing PIN-confirmation screen needs to exist on iOS for MVP, just as it does on Android. Without it iOS users cannot complete a first-encounter exchange.

## What it does (sketch)

- Shows the 4-digit code prominently.
- Lets the user accept or reject the pairing.
- Times out after a fixed window if neither side responds.
